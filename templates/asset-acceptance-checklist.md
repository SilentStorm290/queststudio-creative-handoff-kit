Project: [label]

State options: `pass`, `revise`, `not_checked`. Do not mark a check passed from a thumbnail or from a generation status alone.

## Shared checks

- [ ] Output matches the approved brief's required subject, message and format.
- [ ] Required names, labels and terminology are accurate.
- [ ] The full asset was reviewed in the intended layout or timeline.
- [ ] The editable source and references are identified where appropriate.
- [ ] Rights, permissions and any required credits were checked by the responsible person.
- [ ] Known limitations and the final approval decision are recorded.

## Image

- [ ] Dimensions and crop fit the actual placement.
- [ ] Important details remain recognizable at the intended viewing size.
- [ ] Required copy space is usable.
- [ ] Text, edges, hands, objects and repeated patterns have been inspected where relevant.
- [ ] Captions or text alternatives will be handled in the destination.

## Video

- [ ] Start, transitions and final frame work in the edit.
- [ ] Character, product and scene details remain consistent where continuity is required.
- [ ] Motion and camera direction follow the brief.
- [ ] The entire clip has been watched, including frames outside the selected thumbnail.

## Audio

- [ ] The complete track has been heard.
- [ ] Pronunciation and approved wording match the script.
- [ ] Pauses, levels and ending work in the intended mix.
- [ ] Captions or transcript match the final approved take when required.

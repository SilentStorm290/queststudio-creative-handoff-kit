Use the kit in one short review

  1. Define the destination. Write the intended placement, audience, duration or dimensions, required details and hard limits.
  2. Freeze the input. Assign a script or brief revision such as script-v03. Put direction and approved words in separate fields.
  3. Give each attempt an ID. Record the exact tool or model label and settings you used. Leave unavailable fields blank.
  4. Review the whole asset. Listen to the audio, watch the full clip or inspect the image in its final crop. A thumbnail is insufficient.
  5. Record a decision with evidence. Use pass, revise or not_checked. A correction should identify the affected line, frame or timestamp.
  6. Hand off the selected filename and attempt ID. State which earlier file it supersedes and which checks remain open.

Keep unknown usage unknown

Do not copy an estimate into the recorded-usage field. Leave both fields empty when no usage information was observed. An empty field means unknown or not recorded, rather than zero; do not sum unlike units.

# Asset acceptance checklist

Project: [label]
Brief revision: [ID]
Attempt ID and filename: [both]
Intended placement: [where the finished asset will be used]

State options: `pass`, `revise`, `not_checked`. Do not mark a check passed from a thumbnail or from a generation status alone.

## Shared checks

- [ ] Output matches the approved brief's required subject, message and format.
- [ ] Required names, labels and terminology are accurate.
- [ ] The full asset was reviewed in the intended layout or timeline.
- [ ] The editable source and references are identified where appropriate.
- [ ] Rights, permissions and any required credits were checked by the responsible person.
- [ ] Known limitations and the final approval decision are recorded.

## Image

- [ ] Dimensions and crop fit the actual placement.
- [ ] Important details remain recognizable at the intended viewing size.
- [ ] Required copy space is usable.
- [ ] Text, edges, hands, objects and repeated patterns have been inspected where relevant.
- [ ] Captions or text alternatives will be handled in the destination.

## Video

- [ ] Start, transitions and final frame work in the edit.
- [ ] Character, product and scene details remain consistent where continuity is required.
- [ ] Motion and camera direction follow the brief.
- [ ] The entire clip has been watched, including frames outside the selected thumbnail.

## Audio

- [ ] The complete track has been heard.
- [ ] Pronunciation and approved wording match the script.
- [ ] Pauses, levels and ending work in the intended mix.
- [ ] Captions or transcript match the final approved take when required.

# Hypothetical product-demo handoff

This is a fictional planning example. Lumora, the script, file names and review results are invented for teaching. No model was run, no audio was produced, and the rows are not measured performance or a customer case study.

## Brief

- Project label: `lumora-demo-example`
- Placement: a short product-demo scene
- Audience: a first-time viewer learning where to save a draft
- Language and locale: English, US pronunciation
- Target: 10–15 seconds; hard maximum 15 seconds
- Script revision: `script-v02`
- Approved words: “Open the workspace, choose your draft, and save a version before you make changes.”
- Production direction: a short pause after “workspace”; no extra introductory sentence
- Product pronunciation if used elsewhere: “loo-MOR-ah,” stress on the middle syllable

## Illustrative revision

`VO-01` is marked revise in the example log because its fictional take adds an unapproved introductory sentence. The correction is to remove that sentence and keep the approved words. `VO-02` is marked **not_checked** because no replacement audio exists in this example. It is deliberately not approved.

The tool/model and both usage fields remain blank: no real generation happened, so there is no observed tool label, estimate or recorded usage to enter. Example output paths show how a project might be organized; they are not downloadable audio assets.

## Handoff decision

Not reviewed. In a real project, listen to the entire replacement take, check the approved words and timing, then review it in the video with the intended music and captions. Only then choose approve, revise or reject.
